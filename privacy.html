WAKEWARD

Privacy policy · effective 2 October 2026

SupportPrivacy policy

In short: WAKEWARD does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Nothing about you or how you play leaves your device.

What the game stores

To let you continue where you left off, WAKEWARD saves your progress (your world, where you are in it, and what you've found) on your device, using the device's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party code that collects data.
  • No internet connection is used while you play. Everything the game needs is inside the app.

Purchases

WAKEWARD is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy covers your App Store account.

Children

WAKEWARD collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
