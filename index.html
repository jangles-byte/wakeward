Getting help

Found a bug, lost your way, or something isn't working? Email wakeward.game@gmail.com and describe what happened, which device you're playing on, and roughly where you were in the game.

Common questions

How do I move? Drag the stick in the lower-left corner. Tap the round button, or the label that appears above it, to talk to people or use things. Pinch or use the slider on the right to zoom.

I'm in a hole or against a ledge. Walk into a ledge up to about two body-heights high and you jump straight up. Keep pushing against taller ledges and you climb. If you're ever completely boxed in, lean on whatever is blocking you for a few seconds and you'll scramble over.

Where is my save? Your progress is saved on your device as you play. Deleting the app deletes the save.

How long is it? At least four hours of play, often longer if you wander.

Can I go back? No. Everything happens once.
